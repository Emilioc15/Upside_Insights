# Upside_Insights
# 📊 Behavioral Analytics of Sports Betting Decisions

## 🧠 Overview

This project analyzes **1,891 real sports betting parlays** from the 2025–2026 betting cycle to investigate behavioral and profitability patterns across betting activity.

Using Python, pandas, and data visualization tools, the analysis explores how betting behavior relates to:

* Profitability
* Risk exposure
* Volatility
* Betting timing patterns
* Wager sizing behavior

Rather than focusing solely on betting performance, the project approaches the dataset from a behavioral and product analytics perspective.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

This project was designed to explore several behavioral analytics questions:

* Do high-volume betting periods correspond to weaker outcomes?
* How does odds selection influence profitability and variance?
* Are larger wagers associated with stronger outcomes?
* Does losing behavior influence future wager size?
* How volatile are betting outcomes over time?

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

* Higher betting activity did not consistently correspond to stronger profitability.
* Overnight betting periods exhibited significantly higher profitability variance.
* Larger wager sizes were not consistently associated with better outcomes.
* Wager amounts increased following losses, suggesting potential recovery-driven behavior.
* Profitability was highly volatile, with extended drawdown periods followed by concentrated recovery windows.

## 📚 Analytical Areas Explored

### Betting Activity & Profitability

Analyzed how betting frequency and profitability changed across hours of the day to identify behavioral timing patterns.

### Odds & Variance Analysis

Explored how profitability differed across odds ranges and evaluated the relationship between risk exposure and betting outcomes.

### Sportsbook Performance Analysis

Compared profitability patterns across sportsbooks to investigate potential behavioral or pricing differences.

### Wager Size Behavior

Examined whether larger wagers were associated with stronger outcomes and whether stake sizing changed following losses.

### Volatility & Drawdown Analysis

Tracked cumulative profitability over time and analyzed drawdowns to evaluate bankroll volatility and downside risk.
