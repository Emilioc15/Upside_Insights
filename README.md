# Upside_Insights
Behavioral Analytics of Sports Betting Decisions
Overview

This project analyzes 1,891 real sports betting parlays from the 2025–2026 betting cycle to investigate behavioral and profitability patterns across betting activity. Using Python, pandas, and data visualization tools, the analysis explores how betting behavior relates to profitability, volatility, and risk exposure.

Rather than focusing solely on betting performance, the project approaches the dataset from a behavioral and product analytics perspective, examining how user decision-making patterns may influence financial outcomes.

Objectives

The project was designed to explore several behavioral analytics questions, including:

Do high-volume betting periods correspond to weaker outcomes?
How does odds selection influence profitability and variance?
Are larger wagers associated with stronger outcomes?
Does losing behavior influence future wager size?
How volatile are betting outcomes over time?
Key Findings
Higher betting activity did not consistently correspond to stronger profitability.
Overnight betting periods exhibited significantly higher profitability variance.
Larger wager sizes were not consistently associated with better outcomes.
Wager amounts increased following losses, suggesting potential recovery-driven betting behavior.
Profitability was highly volatile, with extended drawdown periods followed by concentrated recovery windows.

Analytical Areas Explored
Betting Activity & Profitability

Analyzed how betting frequency and average profitability changed across hours of the day to identify behavioral timing patterns.

Odds & Variance Analysis

Explored how profitability differed across odds ranges and evaluated the relationship between risk exposure and betting outcomes.

Sportsbook Performance Analysis

Compared profitability patterns across sportsbooks to investigate potential behavioral or pricing differences.

Wager Size Behavior

Examined whether larger wager sizes were associated with stronger outcomes and whether stake sizing changed following losses.

Volatility & Drawdown Analysis

Tracked cumulative profitability over time and analyzed drawdowns to evaluate bankroll volatility and risk exposure.

Why This Project Matters

Although the dataset represents a single-user betting history, the analytical framework mirrors many of the behavioral and product analytics approaches used within sportsbook and gaming platforms.
